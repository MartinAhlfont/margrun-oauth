# Privacy Policy – Margrun

**Last updated:** October 21, 2026

## 1. Overview

Margrun is a privately operated AI assistant built using OpenClaw. It is intended for personal use by its owner and is not offered as a public service.

This policy describes how Margrun accesses and processes information obtained through Google Gmail.

## 2. Information Accessed

With the account owner's authorization, Margrun may access:

- Gmail messages and associated metadata, including senders, subjects and timestamps.
- Email content needed to identify relevant information and generate summaries.

Margrun does not require access to the owner's Google account password.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Gmail information is used to support personal email management, automated summaries and notifications.

Selected messages may be processed by AI models to produce summaries. Some processing may occur locally, while other configured OpenClaw integrations may use external AI services.

Summaries and notifications may be delivered through Telegram.

## 4. Data Storage and Retention

Margrun runs on privately managed infrastructure.

Configuration files, authentication credentials and operational data may be stored locally. Limited processing records may be retained to prevent duplicate notifications and support troubleshooting.

Retention depends on the configured services and their operational settings.

## 5. Data Sharing

Margrun does not sell Gmail data or use it for advertising.

Information may be transmitted to external services where required by configured functionality, such as Telegram notifications or external AI processing.

## 6. Security

Margrun uses OAuth 2.0 for Google authorization. Authentication credentials are stored with restricted local file permissions.

Access to the infrastructure is controlled by the owner.

## 7. User Control

The owner can revoke Margrun's Google account access through Google Account security settings and can stop the assistant or remove locally stored operational data.
