# Margrun – Personal AI Assistant

Margrun is a personal AI assistant built with OpenClaw and operated privately by its owner.

## Purpose

Margrun helps its owner manage personal information and automate routine tasks, including:

- Reading selected Gmail messages
- Identifying relevant information in emails
- Generating summaries and notifications
- Delivering notifications through Telegram

## Gmail Integration

Margrun uses Google OAuth 2.0 to access Gmail with the account owner's authorization.

Gmail access is used for personal automation and email summarization, not for providing a public email service.

## Privacy

Margrun is intended for private use and is not offered as a public service.

For details about how information is processed, please see the Privacy Policy.
